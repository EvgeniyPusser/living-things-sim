Thermal bench: what inherited experience is worth in a failure

A three-capacity thermal model of a house runs here, in the browser, on measured weather for six cities. Two identical houses are compared: one tuned its own schedule over its first winter, the other started from a schedule pooled over forty houses, each of which had survived a failure of its own.

The left column was tuned just now, on this page: the house made 40 attempts on ordinary days before the failure. The right is the median of the schedules of forty houses, each of which lived its own winter with its own failure. They converged on abandoning the night setback, holding above the norm and reacting more sharply.

What is inside

Model
  • three thermal capacities: air with furniture, envelope, internal partitions
  • heat pump: both efficiency and available capacity fall with outdoor temperature
  • solar gain on four facades from the true solar position
  • 15-minute control step; the physics is solved exactly within each step
Data
  • weather files for six cities, 75 winter days, hourly
  • beam and diffuse radiation kept separate
  • heating power sized by DIN 18599-2
Validation
  • the same model is calibrated against BuilDa, the open Modelica generator by Fraunhofer, validated on two instrumented houses in Holzkirchen
  • agreement within 2 % on heat demand and 0.2 K on mean indoor temperature, across 12 buildings in two cities
